### Why questions 3 and 4 are there

Under Michigan's Home Help policy (ASM 135), a **spouse** or the **parent of a child under 18** generally cannot be the paid caregiver, and caregivers must be **18 or older**. These two questions flag people who likely won't qualify before your team spends time calling them. Keep the "Spouse" and "Parent of a child under 18" choices in the list so you can see who selected them. Don't remove them.

### What the form should NOT ask

To protect privacy, leave these out of the form. Collect them later through your secure onboarding process:
- Medicaid ID numbers
- The name, diagnosis, or health conditions of the person receiving care
- Social Security numbers, date of birth, or driver's license numbers

## Items to confirm before launch

1. **Pay rate:** Confirm that $17.13 per hour is accurate for your agency and the counties you serve.
2. **Response time:** Change "within 1 business day" on the thank-you page if your team can't meet that.
3. **Consent wording:** Have your attorney or compliance contact review the consent checkbox (question 11). Texting rules (the federal TCPA) carry penalties.
4. **Meta ad category:** When you create the ad in Meta Ads Manager, select the **Employment** special ad category.
