Caregiver Ad: Posting Instructions

Michigan Home Help Program recruiting ad, $17.13/hour. Facebook and Instagram, set up in Meta Ads Manager.

Before the ad goes live

  • Signed photo releases from both people in the caregiver photo (and from the client's guardian or representative, if she has one).
  • Confirm $17.13 per hour is accurate for every area the ad will reach.
  • Attorney has reviewed the consent checkbox wording on the Tally form (last question).
  • Tally form tested: test submission received by email and test entry deleted. (Done)

1Campaign

  1. Go to adsmanager.facebook.com and click + Create.
  2. Campaign objective: choose Traffic.
  3. Under Special ad categories, select Employment. Set the country to United States. This is required for job ads; without it, Meta may reject the ad.
  4. Click Next.

2Ad set (who sees the ad)

  1. Conversion location: Website.
  2. Performance goal: Maximize number of link clicks.
  3. Budget: a small daily amount for a one-week test, then adjust.
    Daily budget:   Start date:
  4. Location: our service area. Each location must cover at least a 15-mile radius. ZIP codes cannot be used for employment ads.
    Cities or counties:
  5. Age and gender: leave as set. Meta locks these for employment ads.
  6. Placements: leave Advantage+ placements on (shows on both Facebook and Instagram).
  7. Click Next.

3Ad (what people see)

  1. Identity: choose the First Choice Facebook Page and Instagram account.
  2. Format: Single image. Upload FirstChoice_Ad_Feed_1080x1350.png. If Meta offers to customize the image for Stories, upload FirstChoice_Ad_Story_1080x1920.png there.
  3. Stay on this same screen and go to Step 4 on the next page.

4Add the words (same screen as Step 3)

Scroll down on the same screen. You will see boxes labeled Primary text, Headline, Description, Website URL and Call to action. Type each item below into the box with the same name. The preview on the right side of the screen updates as you type.

5Publish and confirm

  1. Look at the preview on the right side of the screen. Check the Facebook and Instagram versions: the image is clear, the words are spelled correctly, and the button says Apply Now.
  2. Click the blue Publish button (bottom right corner).
  3. Ads Manager returns to the main list. The new ad shows In review. Meta usually approves within 24 hours, and the status changes to Active. The ad starts showing on its own. There is nothing else to click.
  4. If the status says Rejected, or Meta sends an email about the ad, take a screenshot so the problem can be fixed before trying again.

After the ad is running

  • Each sign-up arrives by email with the person's answers. All sign-ups are also saved in Tally under Submissions.
  • Call new sign-ups within 1 business day (the form's thank-you page promises this).
  • Sign-ups who chose Spouse or Parent of a child under 18, or who are under 18, generally cannot be paid caregivers under Michigan Home Help rules.
  • After one week, check results in Ads Manager (link clicks, cost per click) and compare with the number of sign-ups.

Meta updates its screens often, so a button may be worded slightly differently from these instructions.
