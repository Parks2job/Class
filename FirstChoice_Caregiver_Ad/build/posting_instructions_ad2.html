Caregiver Ad: Posting Instructions

"Already Caring for a Family Member or Friend?" flyer. Michigan Home Help Program, starting caregiver pay $17.13/hour. Facebook and Instagram, set up in Meta Ads Manager.

Before the ad goes live

  • Signed photo releases from both people in the caregiver photo (and from the client's guardian or representative, if she has one).
  • Confirm $17.13 per hour is accurate for every area the ad will reach.
  • Attorney has reviewed the consent checkbox wording on the Tally form (last question).
  • Tally form for this flyer is published, the thank-you page shows firstchoicehcmi.com, and a phone test submission arrived by email (then deleted).

  4. Click Next.

5Publish and confirm

  1. Look at the preview on the right side of the screen. Check the Facebook and Instagram versions: the image is clear, the words are spelled correctly, and the button says Apply Now.
  2. Click the blue Publish button (bottom right corner).
  3. Ads Manager returns to the main list. The new ad shows In review. Meta usually approves within 24 hours, and the status changes to Active. The ad starts showing on its own. There is nothing else to click.
  4. If the status says Rejected, or Meta sends an email about the ad, take a screenshot so the problem can be fixed before trying again.

After the ad is running

  • Each sign-up arrives by email with the person's answers. All sign-ups are also saved in Tally under Submissions.
  • Call new sign-ups within 1 business day (the form's thank-you page promises this). Applicants may also call 989-890-1659 directly, so make sure that line is answered.
  • Sign-ups who chose Spouse or Parent of a child under 18, or who are under 18, generally cannot be paid caregivers under Michigan Home Help rules.
  • After one week, check results in Ads Manager (link clicks, cost per click) and compare with the number of sign-ups.

Meta updates its screens often, so a button may be worded slightly differently from these instructions.
