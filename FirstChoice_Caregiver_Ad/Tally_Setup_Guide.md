# Tally Form Setup Guide: First Choice Caregiver Sign-Up

Time needed: about 30 minutes. Cost: free.

Files you will upload (in the `Tally_Uploads` folder):
- `Tally_Logo_400x400.png`: your heart logo
- `Tally_Cover_1800x600.png`: banner photo for the top of the form

Question wording is in `Caption_and_Form_Questions.md` (Part 2).

---

## Step 1: Create your account
1. Go to **tally.so** and click **Sign up** (free).
2. Sign up with a **business email** that only authorized staff can open. Each person's details will be emailed there.

## Step 2: Start the form and add your branding
1. Click **Create form** and choose to start from a blank form.
2. Type the title: **Get Paid to Care for Your Loved One**
3. Hover over the title and click **Add cover**. Upload `Tally_Cover_1800x600.png`.
4. Hover over the title and click **Add logo**. Upload `Tally_Logo_400x400.png`.
5. Under the title, type the intro text:
   > Thank you for your interest in First Choice Staffing Agency. Answer a few quick questions and our team will contact you to talk about next steps. This takes about 2 minutes.
6. Click **Customize** (or **Design**) at the top. Font: **Quicksand**. Background **#FFFFFF**, text **#1B2833**, button background **#E5188E**, button text **#FFFFFF**, accent **#139FDD**.

## Step 3: Add the questions
Tally works like a typed document. Press **Enter** for a new line, then type **/** to open a menu of question types. Pick the type, then type the question.

| # | Type "/" and choose | Question text | Answer choices |
|---|---------------------|---------------|----------------|
| 1 | Multiple choice | Does the person you care for have Michigan Medicaid? | Yes, No, Not sure |
| 2 | Multiple choice | Does the person you care for live in Michigan? | Yes, No |
| 3 | Multiple choice | What is your relationship to the person you care for? | Adult son or daughter, Grandchild, Brother or sister, Other relative, Friend or neighbor, Spouse, Parent of a child under 18 |
| 4 | Multiple choice | Are you 18 or older? | Yes, No |
| 5 | Short answer | What county does the person you care for live in? | (none) |
| 6 | Short answer | First name | (none) |
| 7 | Short answer | Last name | (none) |
| 8 | Phone number | Phone number | (none) |
| 9 | Email | Email address | (none) |
| 10 | Multiple choice | What is the best time to reach you? | Morning, Afternoon, Evening |
| 11 | Checkboxes | (see consent text below) | One checkbox |

**Consent text for question 11** (have your attorney review before launch):
> I agree that First Choice Staffing Agency may contact me by phone, text, or email about this opportunity. Message and data rates may apply. I can opt out at any time.

**Make questions 1 through 9 and 11 required.** Click each question and turn on **Required** in its settings. Leave question 10 optional.

## Step 4: Add the thank-you page
1. At the very end of the form, type **/** and choose **Thank you page**.
2. Type:
   > Thank you! We received your information. A member of the First Choice Staffing Agency team will contact you within 1 business day. Questions? Visit firstchoicehcmi.com.

## Step 5: Turn on email notifications
1. Open your form's **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Email notifications**.
3. Turn on **Self email notifications** and confirm the email address. This is free.

Each time someone submits the form, Tally emails you their answers, usually within seconds. Every submission is also saved under the form's **Submissions** tab, and you can download them as a spreadsheet.

## Step 6: Publish and test
1. Click **Publish** at the top right. Tally gives you a link. Copy it.
2. Open the link on your phone and fill out the form as a test applicant.
3. Confirm that:
   - The form looks right on a phone.
   - You received the email with all the answers.
   - The thank-you message appeared.
4. In the **Submissions** tab, delete your test entry.

## Step 7: Connect it to the ad
In Meta Ads Manager, paste the Tally link into the ad's **Website URL** field and set the button to **Apply Now**. Select the **Employment** special ad category.

---

### Privacy reminders
- Send notifications only to a business inbox, not a personal one.
- Don't add questions about Medicaid ID numbers, Social Security numbers, birth dates, or the health or name of the person receiving care.
- Download submissions regularly and delete old ones from Tally under a set schedule (for example, after 90 days).
