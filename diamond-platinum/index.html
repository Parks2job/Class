Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?
Detroit and the surrounding Metro Detroit area, including suburbs such as Troy, Dearborn, Bloomfield Hills and Ann Arbor. Contact us to confirm your address.
How is pricing calculated?
By square footage and service tier, with a per-visit minimum so every job is covered. Add-ons such as carpet shampooing are quoted separately after a brief walkthrough or photos.
Do I need to be there during the clean?
No. Many clients provide a key, lockbox or access code. Access information is kept confidential, and codes are returned or reset as you instruct after each job.
Do you bring your own supplies?
Yes. We bring our own professional products and equipment. If you prefer we use your supplies, ask about a discounted rate.
Are your cleaners background-checked?
Yes. Every team member is background-checked before working any job on their own, and new hires train on supervised crews first. Crews wear company uniforms and a visible photo ID badge.
What happens if something is not right?
Contact us and a supervisor will acknowledge your concern within 24 hours. If a re-clean is warranted, we schedule it within 48 hours at no additional charge.
What if you find damage in the unit?
We note pre-existing damage during our walkthrough on arrival, and we report any damage or maintenance issues to you with photos before we leave.
Do you offer evening or weekend cleaning?
Yes. Beyond regular hours, we offer after-hours service from 6 PM to 10 PM Monday through Friday and 4 PM to 9 PM on Saturday. Sunday service is available by appointment. Evening slots work well for occupied homes and tight turnover schedules.
What is your cancellation policy?
Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a standard visit, and 48 hours for a move-out job. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee under your service agreement.
