Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
No. Many clients provide a key, lockbox or access code. Your access information is stored securely and shared only with your assigned team.
Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
Yes. Our team arrives with professional-grade supplies and equipment. If you prefer specific products, just let us know and we will use yours.
Are your cleaners background-checked?
Yes. Every team member completes a background check and our training program before working in any client space.
What if I am not satisfied with my clean?
Contact us within 24 hours and we will return to re-clean the areas of concern at no additional charge.
What is your cancellation policy?
We ask for at least 24 hours notice to reschedule or cancel so we can offer the time to another client.
Do you offer commercial contracts?
Yes. We build custom cleaning plans for offices, retail spaces and apartment communities. Request a free walkthrough and we will prepare a tailored proposal.
