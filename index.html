Wheelchair Transport

For riders who use a manual or power wheelchair. Our vans are equipped with hydraulic lifts and secure four-point restraints.

  • Standard, oversized & bariatric chairs
  • Lift-equipped, ADA-compliant vans
  • Securement by trained staff

Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book a ride?
We recommend booking at least 24–48 hours ahead so we can guarantee your preferred time. Same-day rides are often available too — send a request and we'll do our best to accommodate you.
Do you accept Medicaid or insurance?
Coverage varies by plan and location. Contact us with your details and we'll explain your options, including private pay and any programs we participate in. (Update this answer with your accepted payment types.)
Can a family member or caregiver ride along?
Yes. In most cases one companion may ride at no extra charge. Let us know when booking so we can plan for seating and any additional assistance.
What areas do you serve?
We serve our local region and surrounding communities. If you're unsure whether we reach your area, give us a call — we're glad to check.
Can you set up recurring rides for dialysis or therapy?
Absolutely. Standing appointments are one of our specialties. We'll build a recurring schedule so you never have to rebook the same trip each week.
